# SA Days Tracker

Tracks days inside South Africa, outside South Africa and in transit (sea or air), with totals per SA tax year
(1 March to end of February) against the 91-day physical presence test, a PDF summary, your personal and
passport details, and a place to keep PDFs of your ID and passport.

**Open the app:** https://pelorus-capital-projects.github.io/passport-tracker/

## Login

The app opens behind a username and password that you set the first time you open it. Everything it stores in
the browser is encrypted (AES-256) and unlocks only with your password or one of your 8 single-use recovery codes
(shown once, when you create the login: download them and keep them somewhere safe). If you forget your password,
choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and enter a code. The app locks itself after 10 minutes without
use. Each browser and device has its own login, set when you first open the app there.

## Your data

- Nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Days, trips, your profile and your PDFs are saved in the browser you use,
  on that device only.
- Each browser and each device keeps its own separate copy. Use **Personal Info -> Download Backup** regularly,
  and **Upload Backup** to load it on another device or browser.
- Backup files are not encrypted (so they still work if you lose your password): keep them somewhere private.
- This public copy contains the app only: no days, trips, documents, passwords or personal details.

## iPhone

Open the link in Safari, tap Share, choose **Add to Home Screen** and keep **Open as Web App** switched on. Then
open the app from its Home Screen icon, create your login and load your backup there. Always open it from the
icon: Safari deletes website data after 7 days without use unless the site is on the Home Screen.
